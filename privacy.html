GoldenGoose Backup

Privacy Policy

1. Overview

GoldenGoose Backup is a personal, non-commercial backup and recovery utility operated by its owner. It uses Restic and Rclone to back up selected local project files to the owner's Google Drive account.

2. Google Drive Access

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file permission to create, read, update and delete files it uses with the application.

Google Drive authorization is provided through Google's OAuth authentication process. The utility does not require the owner to disclose their Google account password.

3. Data Collection and Use

The utility processes files selected by its owner for backup, verification and recovery.

Rclone uses Google OAuth credentials and tokens locally to access the authorized Google Drive account.

No public account registration or third-party backup service is offered through this website.

4. Data Storage and Encryption

Restic encrypts the backup repository before the backup data is stored in Google Drive. The encryption password is managed separately by the owner and is required for recovery.

OAuth credentials and local configuration files are maintained on the owner's device. Access to the device and its stored credentials remains the owner's responsibility.

5. Data Sharing

GoldenGoose Backup does not sell backup data or intentionally disclose it to advertisers.

Encrypted backup files are stored with Google Drive as the selected storage provider, subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. Retention and Deletion

Backup data is retained according to the owner's backup and recovery decisions. Automatic retention and deletion policies are not currently enabled.

The owner can remove backup repositories from Google Drive when they are no longer needed. Deleting a repository may permanently prevent recovery of the data stored in it.

7. Revoking Access

The owner may revoke GoldenGoose Backup's Google Account access through Google Account security and third-party connection settings.

Revoking authorization prevents further authenticated access until permission is granted again. It does not automatically remove previously stored backup files.

8. Website Privacy

This informational website does not provide a login form, collect visitor-submitted personal information, or intentionally install advertising or analytics trackers.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical information according to GitHub's privacy practices.

9. Updates

This policy may be updated when the utility's features, storage methods or data practices change.

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

10. Contact

GoldenGoose Backup is a private-use utility. Project information is available through its official GitHub repository.
